# Draft data governance statement

This repository analyzes public environmental data describing the Pine Ridge Reservation and hydrologically connected areas. Public availability does not remove the responsibility to represent Oglala Lakota lands, waters, and communities accurately and respectfully.

This wording is a working draft. It has not been approved by the OST Research Review Board (RRB) or Oglala Lakota College Institutional Review Board (IRB).

## Current data boundary

Only public data sources and generated screening-level products belong in the public repository. No OST-collected well, water-quality, infrastructure, household, or operational dataset is included or authorized for inclusion.

Before any OST-controlled data is used, a separately approved process must identify:

- the responsible OST/OLC steward;
- the purpose and expected collective benefit;
- storage location and access roles;
- allowed analyses and prohibited uses;
- review and publication authority;
- retention and deletion requirements; and
- whether derived products may leave OST-controlled infrastructure.

`.gitignore` excludes `data/raw/`, `data/cache/`, and `data/processed/`, but a technical exclusion is not a governance agreement.

## Public data and territorial interpretation

Census AIANNH geometry is used as a reproducible statistical boundary. It is not a legal determination of jurisdiction or a substitute for boundaries and place descriptions defined by OST.

The hydrologic context may include upstream, downstream, or adjacent monitoring sites. Inclusion supports analysis of connected water systems; it does not mean that a site is on OST land or operated by OST. Each configured streamgage includes a written rationale.

Monitoring gaps must be described as limitations in public infrastructure and evidence, not as an absence of water, water use, knowledge, or community monitoring.

## Review and release

Until OST RRB/OLC IRB review is complete:

- outputs are educational and screening-level;
- thresholds are not operational, regulatory, health, or emergency triggers;
- findings must not be attributed to OST or OLC as approved conclusions;
- public release should identify methods, limitations, and data provenance; and
- sensitive interpretations should be reviewed before external presentation.

## Frameworks under consideration

CARE, FAIR, IEEE 2890-2025, Local Contexts, and OCAP® are reference points, not a claim that a particular framework has been adopted by OST or OLC. Their applicability and final wording are deferred to OST RRB/OLC IRB review.
